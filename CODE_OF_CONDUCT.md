# NP2MI Code of Conduct

NP2MI is an unofficial open source project built on Dolphin. We want its
community spaces to remain welcoming, constructive, and safe for users and
contributors of all experience levels.

## Scope

This code applies to participation in the NP2MI repository, including issues,
pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other spaces managed by the project.
It does not make the NP2MI maintainer responsible for independent Dolphin
community spaces.

## Expected Behavior

Participants are expected to:

- be respectful, patient, and considerate;
- welcome questions and different levels of technical experience;
- give specific and constructive technical feedback;
- assume good intent while still taking responsibility for impact;
- respect privacy, licenses, and legal boundaries;
- focus disagreements on ideas and evidence rather than individuals;
- disclose conflicts of interest that could affect a contribution or review.

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behavior is not acceptable:

- harassment, threats, intimidation, or discriminatory language;
- personal attacks, insults, or sustained disruption;
- sexualized language or unwanted attention;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- piracy, requests for copyrighted game files, or distribution of leaked or
  confidential material;
- deliberately misleading technical claims or fabricated test results;
- retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith.

## Reporting

For ordinary technical disagreements, first try to resolve the matter calmly
in the relevant issue or pull request.

For sensitive conduct reports, contact the repository owner, `@Biscince`,
privately using the contact information available on their GitHub profile. Do
not post personal or sensitive details in a public issue. Conduct that also
violates GitHub policy may be reported through GitHub's reporting tools.

The Dolphin project and its moderators are not responsible for enforcing the
NP2MI Code of Conduct.

## Enforcement

The project maintainer will review reports in good faith and may request
additional context. Depending on the severity and history of the behavior,
responses may include:

- a private or public clarification;
- a warning and request for correction;
- removal of comments or contributions;
- temporary restrictions on participation;
- permanent removal from project spaces.

The maintainer will make reasonable efforts to protect the privacy of people
who report incidents. Deliberately false or bad-faith reports may themselves
be treated as a violation.

## Attribution

This document is informed by the Dolphin Emulator Code of Conduct and the
broader open source codes of conduct it credits. It has been rewritten for the
